The Great Barrier Reef: How is it Changing?

Explore where reefs are monitored, how coral cover varies, and where crown-of-thorns starfish are detected. Then examine a recent coral-cover trend.

01 | THE EVIDENCE

First, where do we have observations?

Dots show reefs included in two AIMS monitoring methods. A reef may appear in both datasets. These are surveyed locations, not every reef in the Great Barrier Reef.

Hover over a survey dot to see its tooltip and a magnified map of nearby reefs.

02 | CONDITIONS

Hard coral cover differs between surveyed reefs

Larger circles represent higher mean hard coral cover in 2022-23. Circle size does not represent reef size. Only surveyed reefs are shown.

Hover over a circle to see the reef name and its measured cover.

Nearby coral cover observations

03 | POTENTIAL PRESSURE

Where were crown-of-thorns starfish (COTS) detected?

Each 1° x 1° cell shows the percentage of surveyed reefs with COTS detected in 2023. This measures detection, not starfish abundance or coral loss. Hover to check sample sizes, especially for cells with few surveyed reefs.

Darker cells indicate a higher detection percentage among surveyed reefs.

04 | A MORE RECENT VIEW

What does the 2025 monitoring series suggest?

This line shows the unweighted mean modelled coral cover across 51 consistently observed MMP series from 2021 to 2025. MMP uses different methods and locations from the LTMP maps. The trend is not a reef-wide estimate.

05-06 | PATTERNS AND VARIATION

How much does coral cover differ?

Compare sector-year averages, then inspect variation across shelf positions. Grey cells indicate sector-years with fewer than three sampled reefs or no observations.

Sector key: CA = Cairns | CB = Capricorn-Bunker | CG = Cape Grenville | CL = Cooktown / Lizard Island | IN = Innisfail | PC = Princess Charlotte Bay | PO = Pompey | SW = Swains | TO = Townsville | WH = Whitsunday

Grey = fewer than three sampled reefs or no observations.

The centre line marks the median; boxes show the middle 50%.

Whiskers show typical spread; isolated dots are outliers.

07-08 | ASSOCIATIONS AND COMPOSITION

What else is observed on surveyed reefs?

Compare coral cover where COTS were detected versus not detected.

Each dot represents one reef; horizontal offsets reveal overlapping observations. Dark ticks show group medians. Association is not causation.

Data Sources & Acknowledgements

Monitoring data: Australian Institute of Marine Science (AIMS).

  • AIMS Long-Term Monitoring Program (LTMP) - reef surveys, coral cover and crown-of-thorns starfish observations.
  • AIMS photographic transect monitoring metadata - reef benthic observations.
  • Marine Monitoring Program (MMP) - Coral - inshore reef monitoring and modelled coral-cover summaries.

Data were processed and aggregated for educational visualisation. Monitoring methods and surveyed reefs differ across datasets and years; comparisons do not establish causation.
